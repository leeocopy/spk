Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24/09/2026

Data Controller

SPEAK Corporate English, Marrakech, Morocco.
Contact email: Contact@speak.ma

Data Collected

This website does not use Contact forms, does not collect or store personal data through forms, and does not install tracking or profiling cookies.

The site offers two ways to get in touch:

  • A mailto link, which opens the visitor's email client with the recipient field already filled in. Sending the email is a voluntary action by the user, and the data contained in the message (email address, name, content) is processed solely to respond to the request.
  • A booking button that opens a widget from the Cal.eu service (Cal.com's European infrastructure), through which the user can choose a time slot and enter their own data (name, email, any notes) to schedule a call or meeting.

Purpose of Processing

Any data processed through email or the booking service is used solely to respond to inquiries and arrange appointments, based on the performance of pre-contractual measures requested by the data subject (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Third-Party Services

Appointments are managed through Cal.eu, a service hosted on infrastructure within the European Union, in compliance with the GDPR. For more information, see Cal.eu's privacy policy: https://cal.com/it/privacy.

Cookie

This website does not set its own cookies. The only cookie that may be present is a technical one set by the Cal.eu booking widget when it is opened, necessary for the service to function, and it does not require prior consent as it is strictly necessary (Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code).

Data Retention

Data processed through email or the booking service is kept for as long as needed to handle the request, and afterward according to applicable tax and contractual obligations.

Data Subject Rights

You can exercise your rights under Articles 15-22 of the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) at any time by writing to Contact@speak.ma . You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions
about Speak.

Who are Speak solutions designed for?

Professionals, executives, managers, teams and organizations that use English as a key business tool and want to build precision, clarity, and impact in decisive workplace interactions.

What is the difference between coaching, workshops and events?

Individual 1:1 coaching addresses personal and confidential priorities. Corporate workshops provide hands-on group practice on real scenarios (meetings, pitches). Speak Events are gatherings dedicated to inspiring talks and professional dialogue.

What level of English is required to participate?

There is no standard school level required. Each format is calibrated around your real workplace responsibilities and assessed beforehand to ensure immediate, actionable relevance.

Where do sessions and events take place?

Sessions are held in Marrakech, on-site at your company headquarters, or via interactive remote sessions depending on your team's schedule and location.

What are the pricing terms and engagement options?

We operate with transparent pricing based on the chosen format: individual coaching, workshop/event participation, or dedicated enterprise packages. Contact us for a tailored proposal.

How do I choose the right format for my team?

We start with an introductory conversation to understand your immediate business objectives (board meetings, negotiations, team alignment) and recommend the most effective pathway.
